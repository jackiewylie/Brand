§ 04 — Open Conditions

Human Verification Required

Three conditions were identified during program execution that require human decision or external verification before public launch or commercial use. These are not design issues — they are legal, compliance, and market-validation matters outside the scope of in-house brand work.

OC-01 Powered+ (Tier 05) White-Label Offering — Definitional Conflict

The original D2 brief specifies "full white-label" for Tier 05. Phase 3 design resolution established a "co-brand lockup" treatment. These are mutually exclusive commercial outcomes. Until brand owner and legal counsel produce a written resolution, Tier 05 must not be offered commercially. The Phase 7 partner standards document includes Powered+ specifications for both treatments with the conflict clearly annotated.

⚠ REQUIRES HUMAN VERIFICATION — brand owner + legal counsel sign-off
OC-02 Vigil Trademark Clearance — Class 36/42, US/UK/EU

"Vigil" as a product name for financial data intelligence services requires trademark clearance in at least Classes 36 (financial services) and 42 (SaaS/software) across US, UK, and EU markets prior to any public launch. The in-house brand program has not and cannot conduct this clearance. Engagement of IP counsel is required before the Vigil name and mark appear in any public-facing material.

⚠ REQUIRES HUMAN VERIFICATION — IP counsel, class 36/42, US/UK/EU
OC-03 IBM Plex Mono Institutional Credibility Test (FM-04 / AP-02)

The Phase 4 token system adopted IBM Plex Mono as the primary data/tape typeface, replacing the implicit incumbent. The brand specification (FM-04, AP-02) requires an administered institutional credibility test — showing the product interface to a sample of target users (institutional research analysts, PMs) — documented as PASS before the Phase 5 website and any public-facing product surfaces enter distribution. No substitute for this test exists within the in-house program.

⚠ REQUIRES HUMAN VERIFICATION — user research with target ICP, documented result required
