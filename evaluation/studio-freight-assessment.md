# Studio Freight proposal — assessment against the Boosted.ai RFP

*Prepared July 24, 2026 (partner-selection day). Source: `SF_BoostedAI1_Proposal2.pdf` (192 pp; proposal core pp. 15–24, remainder portfolio/testimonials). Companion document: `proposal/index.html` (the Fair Copy response + phase-one work, for side-by-side reading).*

## Summary verdict

Studio Freight is a genuinely elite **identity-execution** studio — the craft in the proposal itself proves it, the roster (Brex, Perplexity, Mercury, Vercel, Netflix, MetaMask, ElevenLabs) is top-tier, and $287,400 / 10–12 weeks is competitive pricing for this caliber. But measured against **this RFP specifically**, the proposal under-answers the strategy half of the brief: it is a beautifully dressed generic process, and the RFP explicitly asked for the opposite. The risk profile: you would likely get a world-class visual identity and a thinner-than-needed answer to the name, architecture, and positioning questions — which the RFP says come first.

## Gaps against Scope of Work (Section 4)

- **Item 1 (naming audit + linguistic/trademark vetting):** vetting never mentioned.
- **Item 2 (brand architecture with per-tier naming):** absent as a phase, deliverable, or highlight. "Alfa" is never addressed.
- **Item 3 (identity system):** fully covered — this is their core strength.
- **Item 4 (positioning & messaging framework):** partially covered ("brand foundation," plus an annotation about audience-specific messaging). No competitive frame or category-definition deliverable named.
- **Item 5 (handoff: brand book, design token set, messaging playbook compatible with your design system):** style guide + brand guidelines + asset files offered; **no design-token set and no acknowledgment of the "don't touch product UI / cascade into the existing system" constraint.**
- **Your domain question (.ai vs .com / SEO / GEO):** not addressed.

## Small tells

In a $287k document about words: "Multuple Investor Audiences" (section heading), "The mission of this project is build…", "~1 weeks." Trivial individually; collectively worth noticing from a studio pitching verbal identity.

## What they clearly do better than anyone

The physical craft of the document (the engraving illustration system, the typography), the tenets ("drafted with a pencil, not a pen"), the client love in the testimonials — real evidence of an easy, high-output partnership. If the decision were **identity execution only**, this proposal would be close to dispositive.

## Questions to put to Studio Freight before signing

1. Who is on the team, by name and allocation? (RFP required it; it's missing.)
2. How will you test "Boosted" with actual DRIs, and rename-tolerance with institutional clients — or is the name decided in a conference room?
3. Where does brand architecture happen in your five phases, and what does its deliverable look like? What is your recommendation process for "Alfa"?
4. Naming is scoped at 1–2 weeks. Does that include linguistic screens and trademark knockouts in class 36/42? If a shortlist dies in legal, what happens to the timeline?
5. If the evidence says *keep* the name, how does scope and price change?
6. Your handoff lists guidelines and assets — will you deliver a design-token set mapped to our existing system, and do you accept the "brand level only, no product UI" line?
7. What is your answer to the .ai vs .com question?

If their presentation answers 2, 3, and 6 convincingly, the price and craft make them a strong pick — ideally with the SOW amended to name the missing deliverables (architecture model, vetting, token handoff, domain recommendation) explicitly.
