How it works

Three things
most platforms defer.

The gap between a filing and a decision is where intelligence is lost. Vigil closes it.

01 — Monitor
Coverage
without blindspots

Vigil watches your coverage list in near-real time — SEC filings, earnings transcripts, regulatory disclosures, corporate actions. You define the coverage universe; Vigil monitors it around the clock so you don't have to.

  • 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, proxy filings
  • Earnings calls and conference transcripts
  • Regulatory and compliance disclosures
  • Configurable monitoring cadence
02 — Reason
Inference you
can follow

Every signal Vigil surfaces is the result of structured reasoning from primary sources. Not a summary of a summary. The logic from document to conclusion is explicit, each step traceable and auditable.

  • Structured reasoning chains
  • Explicit inference steps
  • No black-box conclusions
  • Audit-grade reasoning logs
03 — Cite
Every claim
to a source

Intelligence without provenance is opinion. Vigil is designed to link every inference to the source sentence in the source document that produced it — an SEC filing, an earnings transcript, a regulatory record. Present it in a board meeting. REQUIRES HUMAN VERIFICATION

  • Sentence-level source linking
  • Document and page citation
  • Primary source, not secondary synthesis
  • Shareable citation packages
