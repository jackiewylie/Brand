# Boosted.ai Rebrand — Phase One

Working through phase one of the Boosted.ai full rebrand (RFP issued July 2, 2026), roleplayed as "Fair Copy," a brand agency responding to the RFP with both a proposal and the opening audit work done early.

## Take two — the current pitch

- **`proposal-v2/index.html`** — **The rebuilt phase-one pitch microsite (supersedes `proposal/` as the RFP response).** One scroll, four visual systems: Fair Copy's own studio chrome (paper/ink/proof-red, Instrument Serif) plus three mutually exclusive creative territories, each section rendered *in* its territory's system —
  - **01 · The Bottom Line** — institutional gravitas: keep Boosted, retire ".ai," endorsed house; parchment/ledger-green/bronze, Libre Caslon, the accountant's double rule as the identity device. Nothing moves.
  - **02 · Calibration** *(the bet)* — intelligence-as-edge: branded house (Boosted the instrument-maker, Alfa Instrument Nº 01); graphite/bone with a rationed signal chartreuse (`#C8F542`), Archivo on a width axis, Spline Sans Mono readouts, the calibrated-scale wordmark.
  - **03 · The Bureau** — full category break: the market's weather service; rename funnel shown mid-flight (Vane ★, Windward, kills included), chalk/slate/storm-coral, wind-barb mark, isobar fields, probability-honest forecast cards and a public scorecard.

  Also inside: an honest audit grounded in the real alfa-ds tokens (`#080808` surface stepping, gold `#C19A6B`, Hanken 300–500), a designed two-axis landscape map (AlphaSense, Hebbia, Rogo, Brightwave, Bloomberg/Kensho, the ambient "AI company" blob) with the open ground marked, a strategic platform ("auditable conviction") with before/after voice corrections, a recommendation with two priced tripwires, and a proposal layer that mirrors the RFP's Section 4/6 language — named team with allocations, 15-week gated timeline, $322,000 fixed fee with a −$29,000 conditional if the evidence says keep the name. Fonts via Google Fonts; no frameworks.

## Take one — prior work

- **`proposal/index.html`** — The full deliverable: RFP response (approach, team, timeline, budget), a phase-one brand audit (the "Boosted" name, .ai/domain strategy, "Alfa," the current visual identity, voice), and three creative directions with palettes, type, wordmark studies, architecture models, voice samples, and honest risk assessments:
  - **01 · The Inheritance** — keep the name, drop the suffix, branded house, dark+gold matured with a daylight register
  - **02 · The Morning Paper** *(recommended)* — full rename (candidates: Lede, Early, Tenor, Basis, Meridian), light-first editorial identity, "never the last to know" positioning
  - **03 · The Two Houses** — endorsed architecture; Boosted/Alfa untouched institutionally, consumer brand ("Tape, by Boosted") in amber-phosphor terminal aesthetic
- **`evaluation/studio-freight-assessment.md`** — Studio Freight's actual submitted proposal ($287,400 / 10–12 weeks) scored against the RFP's own requirements, with questions to ask before signing.
- **`delivery/index.html`** — The full engagement executed Studio Freight–style (Discovery → Strategy → Naming → Visual Identity → Rollout → Delivery), driven to real decisions: six discovery findings, the brand foundation ("never the last to know," portfolio intelligence, the doctrine of quiet), a 24-name funnel ending in the rename to **Lede**, and the built identity system ("the Masthead & the Mark") with logo suite, color, type (Lede Serif / Schibsted Grotesk / IBM Plex Mono), motion spec, applications, and rollout sequencing.
- **`identity/lede-tokens.json`** — The machine-readable token handoff: both registers (product dark / brand light) mapped to the existing alfa-ds role names, plus typography and motion specs.
- **`evaluation/lede-red-team.md`** — Adversarial review of the "Lede" decision: the homophone tax and LedeAI adjacency priced honestly, two fresh naming territories mined (Bellwether replaces Masthead on the bench), and the verdict with legal/market tripwires.
- **`launch/index.html`** — The lede.com concept homepage: the brand proven on its highest-stakes surface. Interactive Morning Lede demo (a lede day vs. a quiet day), verification demo, the doctrine-of-quiet section, pricing posture, and the institutional continuity strip. Re-skinned to identity v2.
- **`identity-v2/index.html`** — **Visual identity, second cut: "The Instrument Line"** (supersedes the v1 "Masthead & the Mark" system in the delivery book). Opens with the honest critique of v1 (one-trick highlighter, Ramp-adjacent yellow, no constructed mark, no motion identity), then rebuilds: the Step logomark with geometric construction, international-orange "Flare" (the flight-recorder color story), a live ambient instrument-line signature, Instrument Serif display type, proofreader's-mark iconography, the STET stamp for quiet days, recut applications, and the 90/8/2 color ration. Tokens bumped to v2.0.0 in `identity/lede-tokens.json`.
