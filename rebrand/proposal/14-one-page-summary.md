## The Four Questions

**Q1: Is there a real brand problem?**
The evidence is unambiguous: a CEO-level incoherence observation, documented dual-name confusion in the sales process, no enforced visual system, and a category claim ("Agentic Investment Intelligence") that exists in our documents but not yet as a coherent market experience. The problem is structural, not cosmetic, and it is costing us in sales cycle length, category clarity, and Series B narrative credibility. Verdict: **Yes — real and consequential.**

**Q2: Does it require outside expertise?**
The AI rebrand work demonstrates that strategy, positioning, messaging, HTML prototyping, and the brand guide can be produced internally at agency quality. What it cannot produce: motion systems and animation, 3D brand language, production-grade logo files and asset libraries, brand film direction, physical applications, and launch campaign execution. These require craft expertise and production infrastructure that is not replicable with current internal resources or AI tooling. Verdict: **Yes — for specific execution deliverables, not the full scope.**

**Q3: Is Studio Freight the right partner?**
Their craft credentials in motion systems, interactive identity, and digital-first brand expression are strong and map well to what we need. The gap: no confirmed financial services or regulated-industry experience in their portfolio, which creates risk of institutional tone mismatch. That risk is substantially mitigated because our brief is execution-only — strategy and positioning are locked and delivered to them as inputs, not for their concepting. Verdict: **Conditional fit — proceed with a tightly scoped brief and explicit financial services review checkpoint built into the engagement.**

**Q4: Is the scope and cost appropriate?**
At $287,400 for the full original scope, we would be paying for strategy, naming, positioning, messaging, digital applications, and brand guidelines that already exist. That is indefensible. A revised scope — motion system, 3D language, refined identity production, brand film brief, physical applications, production asset library, brand audit toolkit, launch execution — should be priced at approximately $170,000–175,000. At that figure, the PR agency termination savings ($180,000/year) fully offset the engagement cost in year one with approximately $8,000 in net savings. Verdict: **Reduced scope strongly recommended — original pricing is not justified given completed AI work.**

---

## Recommendation

Engage Studio Freight at a revised scope of approximately $170,000–175,000 covering execution-only deliverables, contingent on: Studio Freight accepting the AI rebrand output as their locked strategic brief, institutional client validation confirming "The Signal" territory resonates, and Murray confirming PR agency termination timing offsets the payment schedule. This is the path that closes the execution gap, protects the Series B narrative, and ends brand incoherence — at a net year-one cost of approximately zero.
