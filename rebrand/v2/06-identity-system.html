01 — Color System

The Parchment System

The parchment palette is load-bearing. Surface warmth creates native familiarity with institutional document environments — filings, prospectuses, research reports — while being completely novel in the financial software vendor landscape. The single accent (Electric Violet #4718DC) has one function: marking Alfa outputs. It never appears decoratively.

03 — Brand Mark

The Logotype Is the Mark

"ALFA" set in Barlow Condensed Black, fully uppercase, letter-spaced at 0.04em. There is no logomark. There is no symbol. The typographic treatment is the mark — not a placeholder pending symbol design, but the final decision. The brand's authority comes from the word, not from an abstract form associated with it.

06 — Signature Visual Gesture

The Stamp

The stamp is the brand's unmistakable element. A rectangular block defined by a 1.5px solid border in Electric Violet (#4718DC). It appears exclusively when Alfa has generated a specific output. The visual rule is categorical: violet border means the model said this. Everything else has no violet border. The stamp solves a genuine product design problem — visually segregating AI-generated output from editorial context — through brand language rather than UI chrome.

The Categorical Rule

Violet border means the model said this. Everything else has no violet border. The stamp is never decorative. It does not appear on editorial content, user-generated context, third-party data citations, or navigational elements. Violation of this rule does not produce a slightly off-brand result — it destroys the trust signal that the entire visual system is built to create.
