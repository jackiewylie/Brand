## Surface 11: CEO LinkedIn Post (Voice of Josh Pantony)

Last month, a colleague flagged something I couldn't unsee: we were running two completely different Alfa ads on X at the same time. Different visuals. Different tones. Different everything.

A company that builds investment signal intelligence was failing to send a clear signal about itself. That's a problem.

So we fixed it.

Starting today, Alfa is the brand. Boosted.ai — the entity we incorporated in 2017 — stays as the legal and corporate parent in the background. But everything our clients, partners, and investors interact with carries one name: Alfa.

Here's why this matters, and why it took us longer than it should have:

When we founded the company, we were building something the market didn't have a name for yet. "Investment AI" wasn't a category. "Agentic" wasn't a word anyone was using in finance. So we called the company Boosted.ai and the product Alfa and assumed people would figure it out.

They didn't always. And that's on us.

Eight years later, 300+ institutions managing $5T+ in assets rely on Alfa. We process 120 billion tokens a month. We have 15 patents. We went live with Questrade in under 30 days. We have a category now — we call it Agentic Investment Intelligence — and we own it.

What we didn't have was a single, clear name.

Now we do.

If you're an investment professional who's been curious about what we build, I'd love to show you. The platform is the same. The focus is the same. The signal is just cleaner.

— Josh
