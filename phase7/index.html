Brand Architecture

The Endorsed House Model

Vigil by Lede operates as an Endorsed House. Lede is the parent entity; Vigil is the named product. All partner deployments are anchored in this hierarchy. The endorsement line flows from Lede through Vigil to each partner tier — no deployment operates outside it.

Lede

Architecture constraint
All partner deployments must remain traceable to the Vigil product and the Lede parent entity through at minimum a documented audit trail, regardless of whether the Lede name appears on the partner-facing surface. No co-brand lockup, API integration, or white-label configuration may misrepresent the origin or nature of the underlying intelligence platform.

Brand Standards

Co-Brand Lockup System

Two lockup patterns are authorized: the Platform lockup (side-by-side endorsed wordmarks) and the Powered lockup (partner-lead with attributed endorsement line). Each pattern has fixed clearspace, type, and weight specifications.

Pattern A — Platform Tier (Vigil Platform)
[Partner] | Vigil/ Lede
IBM Plex Mono 500, 14–18px.
Separator: pipe character, text-muted color.
Partner name precedes Vigil name.
"/ Lede" endorsement line at 10px 400.
Light register only. Never on product dark surfaces.

[Partner]

Partner name: IBM Plex Mono 700, 18–24px.
Attribution line: IBM Plex Mono 400, 9px, 0.10em tracking, uppercase.
Node: 6px Flare circle (one focal point).
Dark register only. Never on light surfaces.
Clearspace — minimum protected zone around all lockups

Usage rules — applies to all co-brand patterns
— Partner name must use IBM Plex Mono. No substitution of partner's proprietary typeface in the lockup element.
— The Vigil name must appear in its authorized form. "Vigil" alone is permitted at Platform tier; "Powered by Vigil" is the required form at Powered tier.
— Flare (#FF5A14 product dark / #FF4B00 brand light) may not be used in the partner name. It is reserved for the Vigil brand element or endorsed node only.
— Lockups must not be modified, recolored, stretched, condensed, or rearranged. Submit proposed variations to brand@lede.co for written approval.
— The "/ Lede" endorsement line must appear in the Platform lockup and may not be suppressed, scaled below 9px, or rendered below 4.5:1 contrast on its surface.

Governance

Approval Process & Open Conditions

All co-brand applications, lockup modifications, and partner-tier activations require written approval from Lede Brand before launch. The following open conditions must be resolved before the relevant configurations may be activated.

Open Condition · OC-01
Powered+ commercial terms
Phase 1 D2 binding decision ("full white-label, no attribution") conflicts with a co-brand lockup requirement in the Phase 3 architecture. Brand owner and legal must issue a written resolution before Tier 05 may be offered commercially. Responsible: Brand Owner + General Counsel. REQUIRES HUMAN VERIFICATION.
Open Condition · OC-02
Trademark clearance — Vigil
The "Vigil" product name must be cleared by counsel in the relevant jurisdictions (class 36/42, US/UK/EU minimum) before public launch. No partner co-brand lockup featuring "Vigil" may be published or distributed until clearance is confirmed. Responsible: General Counsel. REQUIRES HUMAN VERIFICATION.
Approval Process · AP-01
Co-brand activation
Any new partner co-brand configuration requires: (1) tier confirmation in writing, (2) lockup artwork approved by Lede Brand, (3) MSA with attribution clause signed. Platform tier: 10 business days. Powered tier: 15 business days. Allow additional time for non-standard requests.
Approval Process · AP-02
FM-04 institutional credibility
Before any Platform or Powered partner launch, the IBM Plex Mono editorial identity (FM-04 gate) must have been formally tested and documented as PASS by a qualified external evaluator. This gate is currently open. REQUIRES HUMAN VERIFICATION.
